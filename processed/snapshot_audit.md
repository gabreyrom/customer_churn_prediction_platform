# Weekly snapshot and outcome-label audit

Generated by `build_snapshots.py` from `processed/invoices.csv.gz`. Do not edit by hand.

## Method

- Dormancy threshold D = 90 days. Scoring dates are end-of-day, every 7 days, ending on 2011-12-08 (Thursdays), starting 2010-03-04 (first date with D days of history, as in the dashboard).
- Population at s: customers with a valid purchase on or before s and recency < 90 (still active).
- Features use only invoices dated on or before the end of s: `days_since_last` (calendar days, as the dashboard), `tenure_days` (s minus first purchase day; left-truncated at 1 Dec 2009), `frequency` (distinct invoices), `monetary` (gross £, sum of rounded invoice amounts).
- `crossing_date` = the first day after s on which recency reaches 90: last purchase day + 90, moved forward by every purchase on or before the pending crossing day. A purchase on the crossing day resets the clock. A later return does not undo the event.
- `y_h` = 1 if the crossing falls in (s, s+h]. Empty (censored) when min(crossing, s+h) is after 2011-12-08: 9 Dec 2011 is partial (purchases stop at 12:50); 8 Dec has a normal volume and closes at 20:01.
- Split on s, per horizon h: train s+h ≤ 2010-12-31; validation s > 2010-12-31 and s+h ≤ 2011-06-30; test s > 2011-06-30 and s+h ≤ 2011-12-08. A label's outcome window therefore never passes its period's cutoff, and scoring dates whose window straddles a cutoff are left unused (the gap grows with h).
