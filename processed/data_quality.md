## Definitions

- **Valid purchase invoice**: not a C-prefix invoice; customer ID and date present; every counted line has quantity > 0 and price > 0; at least one product line. TEST*/ADJUST* lines are dropped. Postage, carriage, manual (M), bank charges and discount lines count toward the amount but cannot make an invoice count on their own.
- **amount**: gross sum of quantity × price over the invoice's counted lines. Returns are not netted off.
- **date**: earliest timestamp on the invoice.
- **Rounding**: line amounts are summed unrounded; each invoice amount is rounded to pence once; every total in this report is the sum of those rounded invoice amounts.

## Kept as-is (reported, not removed)

- Identical lines within a sheet: 11,602 rows across 4,268 invoices. Gross amount with them 16,945,042.16, without them 16,888,735.85 (difference 56,306.31). They cannot be told apart from genuine repeat lines.
